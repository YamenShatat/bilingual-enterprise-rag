# Code of Conduct

**Acme MENA Technology — Compliance**
Document owner: Compliance Officer · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 3.2

## 1. Our Principles

We act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect and protect the confidentiality of company and customer information. This code applies to all employees, contractors and board members.

## 2. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to USD 50 from a business partner. Anything above USD 50 must be declared to the Compliance Officer within 5 working days and may need to be returned. Cash gifts are never acceptable.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must declare any personal, financial or family interest that could influence their work. Every employee confirms their declaration by 31 January each year.

## 4. Respectful Workplace

Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated in any form. Employees who experience or witness such behaviour should report it immediately.

## 5. Speaking Up

Concerns can be reported to the ethics hotline on extension 7700 or by email to ethics@acme-mena.example, anonymously if preferred. Acme MENA Technology does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Investigations are completed within 30 days.

## 6. Social Media

Personal opinions posted online must not be presented as the company's position. Confidential information must never be shared.
