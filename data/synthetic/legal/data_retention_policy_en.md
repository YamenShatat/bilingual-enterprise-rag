# Data Retention Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Legal**
Policy owner: Legal Department · Effective: 1 April 2026 · Version 1.2

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how long Acme MENA Technology keeps records and when they must be destroyed.

## 2. Retention Periods

| Record type | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Financial records | 7 years |
| Employee records | 5 years after employment ends |
| Customer contracts | 10 years after expiry |
| Email | 3 years |
| System and security logs | 12 months |
| CCTV recordings | 30 days |
| Backups | 90 days |

## 3. Legal Hold

When the Legal Department issues a legal hold, all deletion of the affected records stops until the hold is lifted in writing.

## 4. Destruction

Records past their retention period are deleted securely, and the destruction is documented with a certificate.

## 5. Review

The retention schedule is reviewed every year in June.
