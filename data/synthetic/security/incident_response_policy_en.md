# Incident Response Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Information Security**
Policy owner: Head of Security · Effective: 1 March 2026 · Version 2.3

## 1. Reporting an Incident

Report any suspected security incident immediately on extension 4400 or by email to security@acme-mena.example. Do not wait to confirm that it is real.

## 2. Severity Levels

| Level | Description | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 Critical | Confirmed data breach or major outage | 15 minutes |
| P2 High | Suspected breach or partial outage | 1 hour |
| P3 Medium | Contained malware or policy violation | 4 hours |
| P4 Low | Minor issue with no impact | Next business day |

## 3. Roles

For every P1 or P2 incident the Head of Security appoints an Incident Commander, who leads the response and decides on communications.

## 4. Handling Devices

Do not switch off, wipe or reinstall an affected device. Disconnect it from the network and wait for instructions from the security team.

## 5. Notification

A confirmed breach of personal data must be notified to the regulator and affected customers within 72 hours.

## 6. Learning from Incidents

A post-incident review is held within 5 working days of closing every P1 or P2 incident, and its actions are tracked to completion.
