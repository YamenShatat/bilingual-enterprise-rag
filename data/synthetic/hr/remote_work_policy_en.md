# Remote Work Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Human Resources**
Policy owner: HR Department · Effective: 1 March 2026 · Version 1.3

## 1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely. Employees on probation must work from the office.

## 2. Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with the approval of their line manager. Remote days must be agreed in advance and recorded in the team calendar.

## 3. Core Hours

Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 Gulf Standard Time on working days, and must respond to messages from colleagues within 2 hours during core hours.

## 4. Security Requirements

All company work outside the office must use the company VPN and a company-managed laptop. Confidential documents must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## 5. Home Office Allowance

Eligible employees receive a one-time home office allowance of USD 300 to purchase a desk chair, monitor or similar equipment. Receipts must be submitted to Finance within 30 days.

## 6. Working From Another Country

Working remotely from outside the country of employment requires written approval from both HR and Legal, and is limited to 30 calendar days per year.
