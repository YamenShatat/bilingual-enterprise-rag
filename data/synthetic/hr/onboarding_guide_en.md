# Onboarding Guide

**Acme MENA Technology — Human Resources**
Guide owner: HR Department · Effective: 1 February 2026 · Version 2.0

## 1. Your First Day

Please arrive at 09:00 at the reception desk of your office. HR will welcome you, confirm your contract and introduce you to your team.

## 2. Equipment and Access

The IT Service Desk (extension 4455) issues your laptop and your building badge on your first day. Your accounts and multi-factor authentication are set up during the same session.

## 3. Mandatory Training

Complete both courses within 14 days of your start date:

1. Security Awareness (2 hours)
2. Code of Conduct (1 hour)

## 4. Your Buddy

Every new employee is paired with a buddy for the first 90 days. Your buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps you settle in.

## 5. Check-Ins and Probation

Your manager holds check-ins with you at 30, 60 and 90 days. Your probation period is 3 months, and the probation review takes place at the end of month 3.

## 6. Payroll

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. Please submit your bank details in the HR portal before the 15th of your first month.
