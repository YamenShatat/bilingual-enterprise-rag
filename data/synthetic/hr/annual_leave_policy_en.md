# Annual Leave Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Human Resources**
Policy owner: HR Department · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 2.1

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how annual leave is earned, requested and approved at Acme MENA Technology.

## 2. Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year after completing the probation period of three months. During probation, leave accrues at 1.75 days per month and may be taken only with written approval from the line manager.

Employees with five or more years of service are entitled to 26 working days of annual leave per year.

## 3. Requesting Leave

1. Submit the request in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the planned start date.
2. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days require approval from the department head in addition to the line manager.
3. HR confirms the request within 3 working days.

## 4. Carry-Over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Unused days above this limit are forfeited.

## 5. Public Holidays

Official public holidays are not deducted from the annual leave balance.

## 6. Unused Leave at the End of Employment

Accrued and unused annual leave is paid out at the basic daily rate when employment ends.
