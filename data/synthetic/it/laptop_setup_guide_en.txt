LAPTOP SETUP GUIDE
Acme MENA Technology - IT Services
Guide owner: IT Service Desk | Effective: 1 February 2026 | Version 3.1

1. BEFORE YOU START
Your laptop is enrolled in the company device management system before it is handed to you. Do not reset it or remove the management profile.

2. FIRST SIGN-IN
Sign in with your company email and the temporary password from HR. You will be asked to set a new password of at least 14 characters and to register your phone for multi-factor authentication.

3. DISK ENCRYPTION
Full disk encryption is enforced on every laptop and cannot be turned off. Keep your recovery key private. The IT Service Desk can retrieve it if you are locked out.

4. REQUIRED SOFTWARE
The following are installed automatically: the company VPN client, the multi-factor authentication app, endpoint protection and the collaboration suite. Do not install software that is not on the approved list in the software portal.

5. LOST OR STOLEN LAPTOPS
Report a lost or stolen laptop to the security team on extension 4400 within 1 hour. The device will be locked and wiped remotely.

6. REFRESH CYCLE
Laptops are replaced every 36 months. A request for earlier replacement needs the approval of your department head.
