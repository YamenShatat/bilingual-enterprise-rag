# VPN Troubleshooting Guide

**Acme MENA Technology — IT Services**
Guide owner: IT Service Desk · Effective: 15 January 2026 · Version 5.1

## 1. Connecting

Open the company VPN client and connect to vpn.acme-mena.example. Sign in with your company email and password, then approve the multi-factor prompt on your phone.

## 2. Common Errors

| Error message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Connection timed out | Check your internet connection, then choose the Acme-443 profile in the client |
| Login failed | Your password may have expired (every 180 days); reset it in the password portal |
| MFA code rejected | Make sure your phone's clock is automatic, wait 30 seconds and try again |

## 3. Session Rules

A session disconnects after 30 minutes of inactivity. Each employee may use the VPN on up to 2 devices at the same time.
