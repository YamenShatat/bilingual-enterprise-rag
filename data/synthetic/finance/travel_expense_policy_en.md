# Travel and Expense Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Finance**
Policy owner: Finance Department · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 4.0

## 1. Booking Travel

All business travel is booked through the travel desk. Trips with an estimated cost above USD 3,000 need the approval of the Finance Director in addition to the line manager.

## 2. Flights

Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more with the approval of the department head.

## 3. Hotels and Meals

| Destination | Hotel limit per night | Meal allowance per day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regional | USD 150 | USD 60 |
| International | USD 220 | USD 90 |

## 4. Receipts

A receipt is required for every expense above USD 25.

## 5. Claiming Expenses

Submit your expense report within 14 days of returning. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days. Personal car use is reimbursed at USD 0.45 per km.

## 6. Advances

Employees may request an advance of up to 50% of the estimated trip cost at least 5 working days before departure.
