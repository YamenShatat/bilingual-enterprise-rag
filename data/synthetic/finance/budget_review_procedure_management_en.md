# Budget Review Procedure

**Acme MENA Technology — Finance**
Procedure owner: Finance Director · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 1.1
Classification: Management only.

## 1. Budget Calendar

Planning starts in September. The Board approves the annual budget in November. Budget owners then review actual results against the budget every quarter.

## 2. Quarterly Reviews

Each budget owner presents a review to the Finance Director within 15 working days after the end of the quarter.

## 3. Variance Explanations

Any budget line that differs from plan by more than 10% needs a written explanation.

## 4. Reallocation

Budget owners may move up to USD 25,000 between lines without approval. Larger movements need the approval of the Finance Director.

## 5. Headcount

New positions outside the approved headcount plan need the approval of the CEO and the Finance Director.
