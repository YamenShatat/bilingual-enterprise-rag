# Employee Handbook

**Acme MENA Technology — General**
Document owner: HR Department · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 6.0

## 1. Welcome

Acme MENA Technology was founded in 2012 and is headquartered in Dubai, with offices in Riyadh and Cairo. This handbook summarises what every employee needs to know. Detailed policies are available on the company intranet.

## 2. Our Values

Integrity, customer focus, collaboration and innovation guide everything we do.

## 3. Working Time

Standard office hours are 09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, including a 1-hour lunch break. The standard working week is 40 hours.

## 4. Leave

Full-time employees receive 21 working days of annual leave per year after probation. The probation period is 3 months. Details are in the Annual Leave Policy and the Employee Benefits Guide.

## 5. Pay

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month.

## 6. Dress Code

Business casual is the standard dress code. Casual dress is allowed on Fridays.
