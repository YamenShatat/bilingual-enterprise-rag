# Compensation Bands

**Acme MENA Technology — Human Resources**
Document owner: Head of Compensation · Effective: 1 March 2026 · Version 2.0
Classification: HR only. Do not share outside the HR Department.

## 1. Purpose

This document defines annual base salary bands by grade for employees at grades G1 to G7.

## 2. Annual Base Salary Bands

| Grade | Minimum (USD) | Maximum (USD) | Annual bonus target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | 18,000 | 24,000 | 8% |
| G2 | 24,000 | 32,000 | 8% |
| G3 | 32,000 | 44,000 | 8% |
| G4 | 44,000 | 60,000 | 12% |
| G5 | 60,000 | 82,000 | 12% |
| G6 | 82,000 | 110,000 | 15% |
| G7 | 110,000 | 150,000 | 15% |

## 3. Annual Review

Salary reviews take place every March. The average merit increase budget is 4% of total base salary.

## 4. Exceptions

A salary outside its band needs written approval from the Head of Human Resources and the Finance Director.

## 5. Scope

This document covers grades G1 to G7 only. Executive compensation is set by the Board of Directors and is not described here.
