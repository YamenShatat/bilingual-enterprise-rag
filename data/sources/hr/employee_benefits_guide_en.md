# Employee Benefits Guide

**Acme MENA Technology — Human Resources**
Guide owner: HR Department · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 1.4

## 1. Health Insurance

All full-time employees are covered from their first day of employment. Cover extends to the employee, a spouse and up to 3 children under 25. The annual limit is USD 50,000 per person. Dental and optical care are covered up to USD 1,000 per person per year.

## 2. Life Insurance

Life insurance pays 24 times the employee's monthly basic salary.

## 3. Leave Benefits

| Type | Entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| Sick leave | 10 days per year at full pay |
| Maternity leave | 60 calendar days at full pay |
| Paternity leave | 5 working days at full pay |

A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Annual leave is described in the Annual Leave Policy.

---

## 4. Allowances

1. Mobile allowance: USD 30 per month.
2. Education allowance: USD 2,500 per child per year, for up to 2 children.
3. Gym membership: 50% reimbursed, up to USD 40 per month.

## 5. Learning Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of USD 1,200 per year for courses, books and certifications. Unused budget does not carry over.

## 6. Employee Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a successful candidate receive USD 1,500 once the new hire completes probation.
