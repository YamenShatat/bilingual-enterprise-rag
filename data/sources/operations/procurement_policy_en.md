# Procurement Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Operations**
Policy owner: Procurement Manager · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 2.5

## 1. Purchase Approvals

| Purchase value | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Under USD 1,000 | Line manager |
| USD 1,000 to USD 10,000 | Department head |
| Over USD 10,000 | Finance Director |

## 2. Purchase Orders

A purchase order is required for every purchase above USD 500. Goods or services must not be ordered before the purchase order is issued.

## 3. Competitive Quotes

Purchases above USD 50,000 require at least 3 written quotes from different suppliers.

---

## 4. New Suppliers

Every new supplier completes a due diligence check by the Procurement team before the first order.

## 5. Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

## 6. Emergency Purchases

In an emergency, a purchase may be made with verbal approval from the approver, and the purchase order must be regularised within 2 working days.

## 7. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must not select a supplier owned by a relative or by themselves without declaring the interest to the Compliance Officer.
