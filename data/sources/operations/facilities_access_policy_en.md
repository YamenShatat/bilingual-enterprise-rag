# Facilities Access Policy

**Acme MENA Technology — Operations**
Policy owner: Facilities Manager · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 1.6

## 1. Building Hours

Employee badges open office doors from 06:00 to 22:00, Monday to Friday. Access outside these hours needs the approval of the line manager at least 24 hours in advance.

## 2. Badges

Wear your badge visibly at all times. Report a lost badge to the security desk within 2 hours so that it can be deactivated.

## 3. Visitors

Visitors sign in at reception and receive a visitor badge that is valid for the day of the visit only. Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times.

## 4. Server Room

Only authorised IT staff may enter the server room. Every entry is logged.

## 5. Parking

Parking spaces are allocated by the Facilities team. Visitors park in the marked visitor bays.
